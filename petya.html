Questions, answered

The basics about Petya and Petya OS.

What is Petya?

Petya is an enterprise platform for selling and delivering digital products, digital services and gift cards from one place.

What can I sell on Petya?

Downloads, licences and courses, fixed-price services, and gift cards in any amount.

What is Petya OS?

Petya OS is the upcoming workspace that brings orders, customers, delivery and reporting together. It is coming soon.

How do I get early access to Petya OS?

Join the waitlist on this page with your work email and we will contact you when access opens.

How do I get started with Petya?
